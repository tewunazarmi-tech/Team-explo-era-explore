About Us

We are Team Explo Era, a local community from Brishyrnot Village in East Jaintia Hills, Meghalaya.

Who we are

We live in the nearby villages, and we spent our childhood exploring the jungle, waterfalls, rivers and mountains of this area. Our guides come from our own community, so the people who take you out are your hosts too.

What we do

We welcome visitors to Krem Chympe and guide them to the waterfall and through the cave, showing them the beauty and blessings that mother nature has given us.

We also try to protect it. By showing visitors and travellers its beauty, we show the value it can give to the community without harming the ecosystem.
